Account Deletion Request

This page provides instructions on how to request the deletion of your account and associated data for the browser of oo1 studio.

1. How to delete your account and data

If you no longer have the app installed and wish to permanently delete your cloud sync account, please follow these steps:

  • Send an email to marcinlowercase@gmail.com.
  • You must send the email from the exact email address you used to log into the browser's sync service.
  • Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".

2. What data gets deleted?

Once we receive and verify your request, we will permanently delete the following data from our cloud servers:

  • Your email address used for authentication.
  • All synced browsing history.
  • All synced app settings (such as UI configurations and hardware-aware metrics).

3. Data Retention and Local Data

Please note that this deletion process is permanent and cannot be undone. Data stored locally on your physical device is not controlled by our servers and will not be affected by this request. To remove local data, you must clear the app's local storage in the settings or uninstall the app directly from your Android device.
